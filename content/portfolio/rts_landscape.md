This is the beginning of a real-time strategy game, currently focused on generating a convincing landscape from modular terrain pieces.

<!--more-->

The generator can take very different tiles—coastlines, cliffs, channels, plateaus, and open ground—and fuse their edges into one continuous surface.  Instead of exposing a rigid grid, neighboring pieces reshape one another so that grass, rock, and water flow naturally across the joins.

The idea is similar to Townscaper.  I wanted a small set of tiles that could reshape one another and still produce a consistent landscape.  The RTS itself is still a work in progress; so far I have concentrated on getting the landscape generator working.
