Recognizing gestures for a spell-casting game idea.

<!--more-->

This was fun, but I wasn't sure how to make it work in a real game.  The prototype captured strokes, normalized them, and tried to match the resulting shape against known gestures.  That sounds simple until you realise every player draws the same rune with a different amount of theatrical panic.  I learned quite a bit about the science of gesture recognition though.
