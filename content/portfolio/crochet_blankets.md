I have been making chunky crochet blankets: soft, tactile projects built from repeated squares, borders and patient evenings.

<!--more-->

Crochet is a lovely counterweight to programming and electronics. The feedback loop is physical instead of digital, and progress turns into something warm and useful rather than another screen full of pixels. I especially enjoy the slow geometry of blankets: rows, corners, repeated motifs, colour choices, and the small decisions that make each square fit the whole.

This blanket uses large granny-square style blocks in muted blues, browns, greys and reds, tied together with a pale border. It has the kind of practical handmade quality I like: simple enough to use every day, but with enough texture and variation that it still rewards a closer look.
