These are two RAM expansion designs from one of my earliest hardware jobs, before I started my electronics degree.  I was working part-time for a local hardware firm and learning PCB design on real products.

<!--more-->

The blue board is a six-layer RAM board for the Acorn Archimedes.  I designed and routed it using Board-Maker.  This particular design was causing trouble because its constraints did not sit comfortably with the limits of the manufacturing process.  I took it home over the holidays, worked through the layout, and figured it out.  The result went on to be quite a successful Archimedes board.

The transparent sheet underneath is the routing artwork for another RAM design, this time for the Amiga.  I designed that one with a colleague.  It is a rather good snapshot of PCB work at the time: a finished multilayer board sitting on top of the full-size routing film.
