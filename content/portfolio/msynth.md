MSynth is a work-in-progress, SuperCollider-like synthesizer for future live coding performances.

<!--more-->

The current prototype can browse and inspect SynthDefs, expose their parameters for live adjustment, build patterns in a step sequencer, track a shared tempo and beat, and play notes from an on-screen keyboard.

I want to be able to explore a sound, change it and sequence it without moving between different tools.  This is still an early prototype, but the audio monitoring and basic synth structure are in place.
