MSynth is a work-in-progress, SuperCollider-like synthesizer for future live coding performances.

<!--more-->

The current prototype brings sound design and performance controls into one compact interface. It can browse and inspect SynthDefs, expose their parameters for live adjustment, build patterns in a step sequencer, track a shared tempo and beat, and play notes from an on-screen keyboard.

The aim is to make exploring a sound, changing it, and sequencing it feel like one continuous live-coding action. Audio monitoring and the underlying synth structure stay visible alongside the musical controls, providing a foundation for the performance tools I want to build next.
