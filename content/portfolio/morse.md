I've been learning Morse code, in order to build and use a transceiver. This is a simple app I wrote to help.

<!--more-->

The idea is simply to bombard you with Morse and have you recognize letters, words, etc. as quickly as possible. It lets you set the character speed and the spacing speed independently, so you can learn the sound of each letter at a decent pace while still leaving your brain a little breathing room. It also keeps going without constantly asking you to click buttons, because Morse practice already has enough tiny beeps in it.

[On GitHub](https://github.com/cmaughan/MornyMorse)
