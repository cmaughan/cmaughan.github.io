Sculpt is a prototype touch-first sculpting application for shaping a 3D mesh directly on a phone.

<!--more-->

With the prototype I can rotate and inspect the model, change the brush strength, work directly on the surface, and undo or redo changes.  A wireframe overlay keeps the underlying geometry visible while the form is being changed.

I was mainly interested in whether sculpting could work well on a phone.  It is still an early prototype, but the basic gestures and undo/redo are working.
